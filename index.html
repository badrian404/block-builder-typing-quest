Before you type

🪑 Sit up tall🦶 Feet flat✋ Wrists floating👀 Eyes on screen🏠 Fingers on A S D F — J K L ;👍 Thumbs on SPACE

Feel the little bumps on F and J? Your pointer fingers always go back there. That’s home base!

FOR GROWN-UPS
  • Play on a computer with a real keyboard. 10–15 minutes a day works better than one long session.
  • Every key is coloured by the finger that should press it — the hands picture shows which finger to use next.
  • Accuracy matters more than speed. Stars and speed-run scores go down a lot when there are lots of mistakes.
  • CAPS + PUNCT (top right) turns capital letters and punctuation on or off in Build School and the Speed Run. Turn it off for an easier start.
  • Once the home row feels easy, turn off KEY LETTERS so he has to remember where keys are instead of looking for them.
  • Progress, stars and saved stories are kept in this browser on this device.
